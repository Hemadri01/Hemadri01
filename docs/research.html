Datacenter load balancing.

My MSc thesis investigated CONGA, a congestion-aware load balancer for datacenter networks. I implemented it in ns-3, compared it with ECMP, and explored Power of Two Choices in path selection. In my experiments, the Power of Two Choices variant did not outperform CONGA. The thesis concluded with a proposal for an RTT-aware approach to flowlet routing, motivated by the goal of achieving effective load balancing without relying on specialized switch hardware.

After graduating, I continued this work, implementing the proposed approach in ns-3 and evaluating it against CONGA and ECMP. Initial results are promising, although the evaluation is still ongoing, especially at larger datacenter scale.

01

RTT-aware flowlet routing

research

An ns-3 implementation exploring RTT-aware flowlet routing as an alternative approach to datacenter load balancing. Evaluation against CONGA and ECMP is ongoing.

C++ns-3.48PythonBash
source ↗
Project notes +
  • Implements several RTT-based path-selection policies using per-uplink EWMA estimates and TCP ACK feedback.
  • Compares algorithms on symmetric and asymmetric leaf-spine fabrics using shared traffic traces, payload accounting, and flow completion time analysis. Current RTT results are preliminary.
  • The prototype relies on TCP feedback. Transport-independent measurement for traffic such as RoCEv2 remains future work.

Traffic generation & analysis

research

Adapted TrafPy to generate traffic workloads and support trace-based evaluation of datacenter load-balancing algorithms in ns-3.

PythonNumPypandasMatplotlibJupyter
source ↗
Project notes +
  • Generated rack-aware and cross-rack workloads for early 100 Mb/s experiments, with CSV exports for ns-3.
  • Analysis notebooks match input and output flows and compare CONGA, ECMP, and RTT policies. Later 10 Gb/s experiments use a separate trace generator.
  • The original traffic generator and benchmark models are credited to TrafPy; local adaptations include plotting compatibility and removal of mandatory GPU initialization from selected paths.
