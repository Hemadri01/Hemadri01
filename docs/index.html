Background & education.

I completed an M.Sc. in Computer Science at Chennai Mathematical Institute in 2026, following a B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honours) from Asutosh College, University of Calcutta.

My current interests are in computer networks, particularly datacenter networking, congestion-aware routing, load balancing, and programmable networks. I have used network simulation and experimental analysis to investigate routing approaches and their performance under different traffic conditions.

Beyond networking, I'm interested in concurrent and systems programming, embedded systems, Linux, and self-hosted services. I enjoy understanding how software interacts with operating systems and hardware, and learning by building practical projects.
